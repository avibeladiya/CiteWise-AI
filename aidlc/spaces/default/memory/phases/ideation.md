# Ideation Phase Guardrails

## Focus

- Problem: high-stakes readers cannot trust uncited LLM answers about their own documents.
- Opportunity: force every claim through retrieval and a citation JSON contract.

## Evidence Standards

- Hallucination in document Q&A is a cited industry problem (RAG papers, financial/legal review workflows).
- Success is not “a chatbot”; success is an answer a reviewer can audit in seconds.

## Scope Discipline

- In: PDF/TXT, extract, chunk, retrieve, cited generate, sources rail.
- Out: accounts, multi-user sharing, embeddings, OCR scans, audio.

## Output Quality

- Intent statement names the user, the pain, and the measurable outcome.
