Acme Technologies Inc.
Q3 2026 Quarterly Financial Report
Fiscal Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
==========================================

Page 1: Executive Summary
---------------------------
Acme Technologies delivered strong third-quarter results, with total revenue of $4.2
billion representing 23% year-over-year growth. This performance exceeded analyst
consensus estimates of $3.9 billion and reflects continued momentum across all three
of our business segments: Cloud Services, Enterprise Software, and Professional Services.

Operating income increased to $772 million, representing an operating margin of 18.4%,
a 340 basis-point improvement compared to the year-ago quarter. This margin expansion
reflects the successful completion of our cost optimization program initiated in Q2,
which reduced headcount by 8% while preserving our highest-priority engineering teams.

Based on these results and our visibility into Q4 pipeline, we are raising our full-year
revenue outlook to $16.8 billion to $17.2 billion, up from our prior guidance of
$16.0 billion to $16.5 billion.

Page 2: Revenue Breakdown by Segment
--------------------------------------
Cloud Services remained the primary growth engine, contributing $1.8 billion in revenue,
up 41% year-over-year. Subscription recurring revenue within Cloud Services grew 38%,
driven by strong retention rates of 94% and net revenue retention of 118%. We added
2,340 new Cloud Services customers during the quarter, bringing total customers to
31,200, an increase of 28% from Q3 2025.

Enterprise Software revenue reached $1.6 billion, up 11% year-over-year. License
revenue declined modestly as customers continued transitioning from perpetual licenses
to subscription models, while subscription and maintenance revenue grew 19%. Our
flagship ERP platform achieved a customer satisfaction score of 87, the highest in
company history.

Professional Services revenue was $0.8 billion, up 6% year-over-year. While this
segment carries lower margins than our software businesses, it plays a critical role
in supporting customer adoption and driving expansion revenue in our core platforms.

Page 3: Profitability and Cost Structure
-----------------------------------------
Gross profit for the quarter was $2.52 billion, representing a gross margin of 60.0%,
up from 57.3% in Q3 2025. Gross margin improvement was driven primarily by Cloud
Services, where infrastructure efficiency gains and higher utilization rates reduced
the cost of revenue as a percentage of Cloud Services revenue from 45% to 38%.

Research and development expense was $630 million (15% of revenue), up 18% year-over-year.
We continue to invest heavily in AI-native product capabilities, with over 40% of our
R&D headcount now focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning initiatives.

Sales and marketing expense was $756 million (18% of revenue), up 8% year-over-year,
reflecting our disciplined approach to growth investing. Sales productivity, measured
as revenue per quota-carrying representative, improved 15% year-over-year.

General and administrative expense was $378 million (9% of revenue), down from 11%
in Q3 2025, demonstrating the leverage in our business model as revenue scales.

Page 4: Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
-------------------------------------
We ended the quarter with $4.8 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term
investments, up from $3.9 billion at the end of Q2 2026. Cash generation remains a
core strength of the business.

Operating cash flow for the quarter was $1.05 billion, representing an operating
cash flow margin of 25.0%. Free cash flow — operating cash flow less capital
expenditures of $180 million — was $870 million, or 20.7% of revenue.

During the quarter, we repurchased 4.2 million shares for approximately $630 million
at an average price of $150 per share, leaving $2.4 billion remaining under our
existing $4 billion share repurchase authorization announced in January 2026.

Total debt stood at $3.6 billion, with no maturities until 2029. Our net cash position
(cash minus total debt) improved to $1.2 billion, up from $0.4 billion at the start
of fiscal 2026.

Page 5: Risk Factors and Outlook
----------------------------------
Key risks to our outlook include:

1. Macroeconomic uncertainty: Prolonged elevated interest rates could reduce enterprise
   technology spending, particularly for large multi-year cloud transformation projects
   that require significant capital commitment from customers.

2. Competitive intensity: The enterprise software market continues to see increased
   competition from both established players and AI-native startups. Maintaining our
   technological differentiation requires continued investment in R&D.

3. Concentration risk: Our top 20 customers represent approximately 35% of total
   revenue. The loss of any large customer could have a material adverse effect on
   our financial results.

4. Regulatory and data privacy: Evolving global data privacy regulations, including
   new AI governance frameworks being considered in multiple jurisdictions, may require
   changes to our product architecture or limit certain use cases.

For Q4 2026, we expect revenue in the range of $4.4 billion to $4.5 billion, representing
21% to 24% year-over-year growth. We expect non-GAAP operating margin of 19.5% to 20.5%,
reflecting continued operating leverage while maintaining investment in our strategic
growth initiatives.
